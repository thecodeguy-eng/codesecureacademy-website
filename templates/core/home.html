How Learning Works

Discover. Enrol. Learn. Practise. Complete. Keep growing.

Every current track is an eight week programme.

  1. Discover

    Choose a learning track.

  2. Enrol

    Review track information and enrol through the registration and payment flow.

  3. Learn

    Take part in structured, tutor-led learning and live sessions, with recordings where provided.

  4. Practise

    Apply what you learn through tasks and projects.

  5. Complete

    Finish the assessments and completion requirements that apply.

  6. Keep Growing

    Continue learning and stay part of the community.

Why Code Secure Academy

Learning should lead somewhere.

Access to information is not enough. Learners need structure, room to practise, useful guidance and a clearer sense of what to do next.

Code Secure Academy brings guided learning and practical work together, and is building a wider ecosystem to support continued growth after the programme.
