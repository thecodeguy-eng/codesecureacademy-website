About us

Training people to actually build things

Code Secure Academy runs cohort-based, hands-on training in Frontend Development, Backend Development, Cybersecurity, and Graphic Design, built around one goal: you finish with something real you actually built.

We started this because too much online education ends the same way: a finished playlist, a certificate nobody asks for, and no actual project to show for it. A cohort changes that, fixed dates, a group of people on the same timeline, and a track that ends with something you built yourself.

Our Team

The people behind it

Ugochukwu Victory Emmanuel

Founder and Academy Director

Victory is the founder and Academy Director of Code Secure Academy, focused on ethical hacking and cybersecurity.

He studied Information Technology at the Federal University of Technology Owerri, where he built and secured many real projects. When he was learning tech himself, he noticed that people who had been in the field for years were still relying on gigs or struggling to find work. That led him to ask a question he couldn't leave alone: what next after learning? Code Secure Academy is his answer. It's a place to learn alongside others, build real projects, and leave knowing how to turn your skills into income, with security built into everything you make.

How we teach

A real curriculum, not a content dump

Every track runs as an 8-week cohort with a fixed start and end date, worked through alongside the same group of people from week one to graduation. Expect to put in roughly 6-10 hours a week, split between live sessions with your cohort and self-paced lessons on your dashboard, with a new project most weeks and one bigger, portfolio-ready project added right before you graduate.

  • ✓8 weeks, fixed dates. A real start and end, so the course can't quietly stall out halfway through.
  • ✓Live + self-paced. Live sessions with your cohort, plus lessons on your dashboard you work through at your own pace between them.
  • ✓Weekly projects, plus a final one. You build something most weeks, then finish with one larger, portfolio-ready project that pulls it all together.
