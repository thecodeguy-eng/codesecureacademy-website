About us

A Nigerian technology learning and innovation ecosystem.

Code Secure Academy is a Nigerian technology learning and innovation ecosystem for beginners and people with existing experience who want to improve.

Code Secure Academy is an academy brand of Code Secure Enterprise Limited.

Explore Learning Tracks Partner with us
The problem we address

Between learning and knowing what to do next

The central problem is the gap between consuming learning material and knowing how to apply a skill, practise it, improve and decide what to do next. Code Secure Academy brings guided learning, practical work and a continuing community together to help close that gap.

Our philosophy
Learning should not be limited by financial barriers, and growth should not end at graduation.
Vision

To become a globally recognised technology learning and innovation ecosystem that makes quality technology education accessible and helps people participate meaningfully in the digital economy.

Mission

To provide affordable, practical and accessible technology education, while building pathways to experience, resources, community, career development and continued growth.

Purpose

To reduce the barriers that keep people from entering and progressing in technology, especially financial barriers and the gap between learning a skill and knowing what to do next.

Our team

The people behind it

Ugochukwu Victory Emmanuel

Founder and Academy Director

Victory is the founder and Academy Director of Code Secure Academy, focused on ethical hacking and cybersecurity.

He studied Information Technology at the Federal University of Technology Owerri, where he built and secured many real projects. When he was learning tech himself, he noticed that people who had been in the field for years were still relying on gigs or struggling to find work. That led him to ask a question he couldn't leave alone: what next after learning? Code Secure Academy is his answer. It's a place to learn alongside others, build real projects, and leave knowing how to turn your skills into income, with security built into everything you make.

Ibeawuchi C.N.

Co-Founder and Chief Technology Officer

Ibeawuchi is a full stack developer, mostly in Django, and the co-founder and CTO of Code Secure Academy. He believes in what CSA is building and owns the technical side of making it work.
