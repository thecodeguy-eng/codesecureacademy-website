Cookie Policy

Last updated: {% now "F j, Y" %}

Cookies are small files stored in your browser. Here's what we use them for, and how to change your choice at any time.

Strictly necessary (always on)

These keep the site functioning and can't be switched off: staying logged in, remembering your cart/checkout state, and protecting forms against CSRF attacks. Without these, login and payment simply wouldn't work.

Preferences

One small cookie/local-storage entry remembers the cookie choice you make below, so we don't ask you every visit.

Analytics (off by default)

Reserved for future use (e.g. Google Analytics) to understand how the site is used. Nothing in this category is active yet. If we turn it on later, it will stay off for you until you opt in.

Marketing (off by default)

Reserved for future use (e.g. retargeting/ad pixels). Nothing in this category is active yet, and it will stay off until you opt in.

Managing your choice

Use the cookie banner on your first visit, or click "Manage cookie preferences" in the footer any time to accept, reject, or change your choice.
